# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-20

- Dataset of 276 traced facts for Novartis, GSK and Novo Nordisk, fiscal years 2023 to 2025, from SEC 20-F statement pages, with ECB rates, a risk-free rate and an equity risk premium.
- Reconciliation to the SEC XBRL facts API (39 of 39), balance sheet identities, and model tests.
- Comparable-company dashboard, Novartis DCF with an itemised bridge, two sensitivity heatmaps, searchable dataset, Excel model with live formulas.
- No share prices by design; multiples appear only from user-entered prices.
