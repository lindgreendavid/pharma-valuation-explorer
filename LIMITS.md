# Limits

The full list is in `docs/methodology.md` and the Limits section of the site. In short: three companies and three years; reported IFRS figures, not adjusted; no share prices; a simplified enterprise-to-equity bridge that omits litigation and tax provisions, off-balance-sheet commitments and pension surpluses; DCF defaults are the author's assumptions and terminal value is about four fifths of enterprise value; no external audit of the dataset. Not investment advice.
