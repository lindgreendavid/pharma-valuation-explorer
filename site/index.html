What this is for

The problem

Pharma financials sit in long filings. Comparisons go wrong when currencies, definitions and reporting treatments are mixed without saying so.

The user

An analyst comparing pharmaceutical companies who needs to inspect the evidence, understand the assumptions and see a valuation range.

The decision

Which like-for-like measures to trust, and what a reasonable value range looks like once the assumptions are explicit.

Limits: what this does not show.

Read these before the numbers. They are part of the result.

  • Three companies, three years. A comparison of three large IFRS reporters. It says nothing about the sector or about companies that report differently. Sanofi was left out of this release because its 2025 statements carry a major discontinued operation; it is a candidate for the next release.
  • No market prices. No public share-price source with a clear reuse licence was available programmatically, so prices are never invented. Multiples and the comparison with market value appear only when you enter a price.
  • Reported, not adjusted. Figures are as filed under IFRS. Company "core" or "adjusted" measures are not used, so impairments, restructuring and acquisition effects are inside the reported margins. EV/EBITDA is not shown because depreciation and amortisation are not separately presented on the face of all three statements.
  • The bridge is not complete. It covers borrowings and leases, contingent consideration, cash, investments, post-employment liabilities and non-controlling interests. It omits litigation and tax provisions, off-balance-sheet commitments, pension surpluses and commitments outside IFRS 16. GSK's non-controlling interests have a negative book value, so an earnings-multiple option is offered.
  • Assumptions drive the value. Growth, margins, tax, beta and the capital structure are the author's assumptions. Terminal value is about four fifths of enterprise value. A small change in WACC or terminal growth moves the result a lot, which is what the heatmaps show.
  • Small, hand-mapped dataset. About 270 facts, mapped line by line and reconciled to a second extraction path, but not audited by anyone else. Share counts are cover-page figures at the filing date.
